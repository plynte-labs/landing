Today we published the public **Plynte Labs** hub — the home of our open-source
ecosystem. From here you can follow every project, understand our mission, and
join in to contribute.

## What you'll find

- **Ecosystem:** Brick Draw and LiveAudio are already live; OpenCohost is coming
  soon.
- **How we build:** local first, MIT license, CI/CD, and hardware acceleration.
- **Contribute:** fork, branch, Pull Request with conventional commits, review.

This is just the beginning. Forge, create, share. No strings attached.
